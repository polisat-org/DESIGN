Mission Hermes

The platform

A 2U CubeSat, 10 × 10 × 22.7 cm, built around six subsystems.

Payload

ADS-B receiver: patch antenna, RTL-SDR V3, Raspberry Pi Zero W

On-board computer

STM32L476RG running a five-state mission state machine

Communication

LoRa 433 MHz, half-duplex, with a deployable antenna

Attitude control

Reaction wheel, gyroscope, magnetometer and light sensors

Power

Solar panels, two 18650 cells, 3.3, 5 and 13.8 V buses

Structure

Aluminium 7075 frame with radiation shielding
