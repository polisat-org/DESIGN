Inside the satellite

Six subsystems in a 2U frame. The team designed and integrated each of them.

Payload

Custom 1090 MHz patch antenna, RTL-SDR V3 and Raspberry Pi Zero W

Structure

Aluminium 7075 frame, checked against random vibration

Power

Solar panels, two 18650 cells and battery heating

Communication

LoRa 433 MHz radio with quarter-wave monopoles

On-board computer

STM32L476RG running the mission state machine

Attitude control

Reaction wheel to slow the satellite’s spin

Mission

HERMES

2U CubeSat
ADS-B receiver
