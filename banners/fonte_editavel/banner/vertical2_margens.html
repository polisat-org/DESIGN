The numbers behind the design

Before building anything, we checked how the satellite would hold up. These values come from our own analyses.

27.5dB
Link margin between aircraft and satellite at 1090 MHz, with our patch antenna designed in Ansys HFSS
24.9dB
Link margin on the LoRa 433 MHz downlink to the ground station
2.44
Lowest structural safety factor under the 14.1 g RMS random vibration profile
<100rad
Radiation dose on the electronics over the mission, with a 2× design margin
