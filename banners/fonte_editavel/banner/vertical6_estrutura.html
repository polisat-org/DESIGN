Hermes · Structure and power

Structure, power and the space environment

Structure

2U frame in aluminium 7075, analysed for the 14.1 g RMS random-vibration profile on all three axes.

Power

Solar panels and two 18650 cells. Regulated 3.3, 5 and 13.8 V buses, with current and charge monitored.

Radiation

Simulated in SPENVIS for four years at about 500 km, with shielding around the electronics.

Thermal

Battery heaters keep the cells in range. A thermal-vacuum bake-out is planned to remove volatile materials.
